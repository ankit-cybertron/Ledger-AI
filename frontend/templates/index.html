About Ledger AI

Ledger AI is an enterprise financial reconciliation platform designed for absolute transparency. Traditional reconciliation relies on manual spreadsheet formulas or black-box AI models that force-match unbalanced rows. Ledger AI introduces a Deterministic 3-Pass Cascade Engine: exact mathematical rules execute first, composite similarity matrix scores candidates second, batch fee equations resolve payouts third, and Groq Llama-3 LLM reasoning evaluates genuine ambiguities with audit trails.

Multi-Format Data Ingestion

Parses raw CSVs, Excel sheets, and PDF statement tables with Levenshtein column mapping, duplicate header resolution, and SHA-256 deduplication hashing.

Deterministic 3-Pass Engine

Matches exact UTR reference keys, evaluates 4-factor composite similarity scores ($S = 0.40 \cdot \text{ID} + 0.30 \cdot \text{Amt} + \dots$), and solves batch payout fee equations.

Groq Llama-3 Agent & Comparison Drawer

Deep semantic AI analysis for ambiguous names and non-standard text. Side-by-side comparison window highlights matching keywords and net variance.

Corporate PDF Audit Reports

Generates executive-ready PDF audit reports featuring summary KPI tiles, status-coded badges, standardized currency formatting, and itemized match tables.
