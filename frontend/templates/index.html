About Ledger AI

Ledger AI is built to redefine financial reconciliation for modern businesses. Traditional reconciliation relies on tedious spreadsheets or unpredictable black-box AI algorithms. Ledger AI introduces a deterministic Rules-First, AI-Second engine: strict mathematical rules execute first, ML transposition detection handles minor numerical swaps, and LLM reasoning steps in only for genuine ambiguities.

Deterministic Accuracy

Every match is backed by clear, auditable rule logic. No force-matched rows or AI hallucinations.

Smart Exception Resolution

Ask questions in plain language to resolve discrepancies, classify variances, and close financial periods effortlessly.

Audit-Locked Periods

Lock reconciled financial periods to generate permanent, tamper-proof audit trails for accounting compliance.
