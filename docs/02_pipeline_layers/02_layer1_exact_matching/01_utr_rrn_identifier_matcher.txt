===============================================================================
STAGE 02 - LAYER 1: EXACT MATCHING | STEP 01: UTR & RRN IDENTIFIER MATCHER
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Scans primary and counterpart records for exact matches on unique banking reference 
codes, including UTR (Unique Transaction Reference), RRN (Retrieval Reference 
Number), Bank Ref IDs, Order IDs, and Bill numbers.

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Banking networks (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI) transmit standardized UTR codes across 
participating financial institutions. An exact UTR match between bank and gateway 
provides definitive proof of payment.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves the primary matching workload deterministically without expensive ML or 
LLM processing, achieving high performance for standard transactions.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Instantly resolves 70-85% of standard commercial transactions with 100% confidence.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Executed in Pass 1 of the 5-layer reconciliation pipeline.
