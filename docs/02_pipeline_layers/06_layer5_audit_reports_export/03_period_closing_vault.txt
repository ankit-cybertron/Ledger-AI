===============================================================================
STAGE 06 - LAYER 5: REPORTS & EXPORT | STEP 03: PERIOD CLOSING & AUDIT VAULT
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Locks the active reconciliation run, marks it as CLOSED, generates SHA-256 
checksums of all input/output files, and stores the snapshot into the Period 
Audit Vault (`data/vault/<period_name>/`).

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Financial periods must be formally closed to prevent retro-active changes or 
accidental overwrites of past months' accounting records.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves audit compliance requirements by ensuring past financial periods remain 
immutable and verifiable.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Provides historical audit history and allows finance teams to inspect past 
closed runs at any time.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Executed when the user clicks "Close Financial Period" in the Reports panel.
