===============================================================================
STAGE 06 - LAYER 5: REPORTS & EXPORT | STEP 01: REPORTLAB EXECUTIVE PDF GENERATOR
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Compiles an executive-ready, multi-page PDF audit report using ReportLab's 
Platypus flowable framework. It includes executive KPI tiles, status breakdown 
tables, itemized match logs, exception summary lists, and an integrity audit.

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Finance leaders, controllers, and external auditors require formal, immutable 
PDF documentation for period-end sign-offs and regulatory compliance.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves the problem of manually building presentation decks or printing web pages 
to report monthly reconciliation results.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Generates print-ready PDFs with page numbers ("Page X of Y"), branded headers, 
and zero-loss mathematical proof equations.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Executed in Layer 5 or when the user clicks "Download Executive PDF Audit" in the 
Reports panel.
