===============================================================================
STAGE 04 - LAYER 3: ML COMPOSITE SCORER | STEP 03: WEEKEND & BUSINESS DAY OFFSET
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Adjusts date decay scoring based on business calendar days. Saturday and Sunday 
are treated as non-settlement days, so Friday-to-Monday transactions are scored 
with 0 calendar day penalty.

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Banking settlement systems (NEFT/RTGS/ACH) do not process payouts on weekend 
holidays. A strict 3-calendar-day penalty would unfairly penalize Friday payments 
settled on Monday.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Prevents artificial score degradation for weekend and bank holiday payments.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Maintains high confidence score precision across calendar weekends.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Evaluated during date proximity scoring in Layer 3.
