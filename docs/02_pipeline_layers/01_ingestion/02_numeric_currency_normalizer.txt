===============================================================================
STAGE 01 - INGESTION | STEP 02: NUMERIC & CURRENCY NORMALIZER
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Converts non-standard monetary string representations into standardized 64-bit 
floating-point numbers. It handles:
  - SBI Parenthetical Debits: `(1,234.56)` -> `-1234.56`
  - Suffix Dr/Cr Indicators: `1234.56 Dr` -> `-1234.56`, `1234.56 Cr` -> `1234.56`
  - Currency Symbols: Strips `₹`, `$`, `€`, `£`, commas, and whitespace.
  - Separate Debit/Credit Columns: Merges separate "Deposit" and "Withdrawal" 
    columns into a unified net signed amount column.

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Financial institutions represent negative numbers and currencies differently. 
Comparing amounts across statements requires signed numeric values (positive for 
inflows/deposits, negative for outflows/payments).

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Eliminates parsing crashes and arithmetic errors caused by currency symbols, 
thousands separators, or accounting parenthetical notation.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Ensures amount comparison, variance calculation, and fee equations operate 
on clean numeric values.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Applied to every row extracted during statement parsing.
