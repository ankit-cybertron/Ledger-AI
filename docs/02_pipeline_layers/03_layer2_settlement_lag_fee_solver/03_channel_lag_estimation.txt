===============================================================================
STAGE 03 - LAYER 2: FEE SOLVER | STEP 03: CHANNEL SETTLEMENT LAG ESTIMATION
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Estimates channel-specific settlement lags based on payment instrument:
  - UPI / IMPS: T+0 (Same-day settlement)
  - Razorpay / PayU: T+2 business days
  - International Cards / Stripe: T+3 to T+5 business days

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Customer payments collected on Friday might not hit the bank account until 
Tuesday. Without lag estimation, date-matching algorithms fail due to weekend 
or holiday offsets.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves date mismatch issues for weekend transactions and holiday batches.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Provides accurate date alignment for liquidity modeling and cash forecasting.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Applied when matching batch payout dates against bank deposit dates.
