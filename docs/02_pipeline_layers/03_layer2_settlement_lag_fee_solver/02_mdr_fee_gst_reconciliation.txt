===============================================================================
STAGE 03 - LAYER 2: FEE SOLVER | STEP 02: MDR FEE & GST RECONCILIATION
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Solves the exact mathematical fee equation for payment processor payouts:
  Net Bank Deposit = Sum(Gross Orders) - MDR Commission Fees - 18% GST Tax

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Payment gateways deduct 1.5% - 2.5% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) plus 18% GST 
on fees before remitting net cash into the bank account. Net deposits never equal 
gross customer sales.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves amount variances caused by fee deductions, preventing net deposits from 
being flagged as unmatched discrepancies.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Automatically verifies fee structures and isolates fee variances into 
transparent fee ledger accounts.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Evaluated when comparing net bank deposits against gross gateway batch totals.
