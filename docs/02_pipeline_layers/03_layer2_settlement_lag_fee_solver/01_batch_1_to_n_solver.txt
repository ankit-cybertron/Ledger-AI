===============================================================================
STAGE 03 - LAYER 2: FEE SOLVER | STEP 01: 1-TO-N BATCH PAYOUT SOLVER
===============================================================================

1. WHAT IT DOES:
Reconciles 1 lump-sum bank deposit against N individual order transactions 
captured by payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe, Paytm).

2. WHY IT DOES IT:
Payment processors do not deposit funds transaction-by-transaction. Instead, 
they aggregate hundreds of customer transactions into a single daily or 
weekly net batch payout deposit into the merchant's bank account.

3. WHAT IT SOLVES:
Solves the many-to-one reconciliation problem where a single bank deposit 
amount (e.g., ₹98,500.00) equals the sum of 50 individual orders minus payment 
gateway MDR commissions.

4. HOW IT IS USEFUL:
Eliminates manual spreadsheet reconciliation where accountants sum order lines 
to match bank deposit entries.

5. WHEN IT IS USED:
Executed in Layer 2 after Pass 1 exact 1-to-1 UTR matching completes.
