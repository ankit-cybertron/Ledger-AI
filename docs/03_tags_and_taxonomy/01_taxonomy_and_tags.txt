===============================================================================
LEDGER AI - RECONCILIATION TAXONOMY & STATUS TAG SPECIFICATION
===============================================================================

1. SETTLED TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied exclusively when a transaction match involves at least one designated 
Primary Statement record (such as a Bank Account, Cash Ledger, or Treasury Feed) 
and satisfies either 100% amount parity (Pass 1 UTR match) or a valid net batch 
MDR fee equation (Pass 3 Batch Solver).

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Signals to the finance team that real liquid cash has successfully arrived or 
departed from the company's bank account. Records tagged as SETTLED are 
automatically committed to liquid cash balance totals and cleared from open 
sub-ledger tracking.


2. MATCHED TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied when a valid, high-confidence match (Composite Score >= 0.85 or LLM 
confirmed) is established strictly between two Counterpart statement sources 
(e.g., Payment Gateway Settlement Feed vs E-Commerce Order Book, or Gateway vs 
UPI Register), without a Primary Bank Statement record directly involved in 
that specific pairing.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Indicates sub-ledger alignment between operational systems prior to bank deposit. 
It confirms that gateway collections match order transactions, isolating any 
in-transit delays before funds land in the main bank account.


3. SIMILAR TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied when a candidate match yields a composite score in the intermediate 
confidence window (0.50 <= Score < 0.85). This occurs when transaction amounts 
or reference strings align partially, but minor date offsets, fuzzy narration 
differences, or untracked fee variances exist.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Prevents automated force-matching while routing potential candidate pairs into the 
Review Drawer. Finance users or the Groq LLM agent can review the shared feature 
highlights and explicitly confirm or reject the match with one click.


4. UNMATCHED TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied to fresh, freshly ingested transaction records before auto-matching, or 
records that fail all matching layers with a composite score below 0.50.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Establishes the initial baseline for newly imported data and isolates open 
discrepancies. Summing all UNMATCHED amounts calculates the Net Variance KPI 
for the period. UNMATCHED records are populated into the Exception Ledger for 
LLM reasoning or manual review.


5. INTERNATIONAL TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied to transactions originating from foreign bank accounts, international 
credit cards, or cross-border payment gateways (e.g., Stripe USD/EUR transactions 
settling in INR).

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Triggers multi-currency conversion logic and adjusts settlement lag expectations 
(T+3 to T+5 days instead of domestic T+2). It allows foreign exchange (FX) rate 
variances to be separated from standard operational MDR fees.


6. ROUND_OFF_VARIANCE TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied when transaction amounts match within a tiny numerical rounding 
tolerance (e.g., <= ₹1.00 or $0.05 difference) caused by currency conversion 
rounding or fractional GST calculations.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Allows the system to automatically pair transactions without rejecting them over 
sub-cent rounding noise, posting the micro-difference to a designated Rounding 
Variance ledger account.


7. FEE_DEDUCTED TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied to batch payout settlements where the net bank deposit is less than the 
gross sum of individual orders due to payment processor MDR commission fees 
and 18% GST tax.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Informs the reporting engine that the amount delta is not an uncollected loss 
or missing payment, but rather an authorized merchant service fee expense.


8. HIGH_CONFIDENCE TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied to candidate pairs achieving a multi-vector similarity score >= 0.95 or 
verifiable UTR identifier equality.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Bypasses manual audit queues and auto-commits the match, enabling straight-through 
processing (STP) for clean commercial transactions.


9. EXCEPTION TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied to transactions flagged for human investigation due to structural 
anomalies, such as missing bank statement counterpart entries, duplicate 
payments, or chargeback reversals.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Isolates high-risk line items into the Exception Ledger drawer, preventing them 
from distorting period-end financial balance sheets until resolved.


10. UNRECONCILED TAG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Used:
Applied when an auditor manually unlinks a previously matched pair or overrides 
a auto-settled status back to an open state during an audit review.

How It Affects Decision-Making:
Re-opens the transaction pair for fresh matching evaluation, resetting all 
downstream KPI calculations and report summaries.
