===============================================================================
STAGE 07 - TECHNICAL MECHANICS | STEP 02: TWO-PASS PDF GENERATION & CHART STREAMING
===============================================================================

1. HOW WE DO IT:
Executive PDF reports are generated using ReportLab's `SimpleDocTemplate` combined 
with a custom `NumberedCanvas` class that intercepts canvas page drawing calls in 
a two-pass evaluation.

2. WHY WE DO IT:
Standard single-pass PDF generators cannot know the total page count until document 
rendering finishes. Header footers requiring "Page X of Y" formatting fail or require 
hardcoded numbers.

3. MECHANICS:
  - Two-Pass NumberedCanvas: Pass 1 records all page operations without writing the 
    footer. When the document completes, Pass 2 retrieves the final total page count `_pageNumber` 
    and writes running headers/footers ("Page X of Y") onto every page stream.
  - In-Memory Matplotlib Chart Streaming: Visual donut and variance charts are drawn 
    using Matplotlib in headless mode (`matplotlib.use('Agg')`), saved into a 
    `io.BytesIO()` binary RAM buffer, and passed directly to ReportLab's `Image` 
    flowable constructor without touching disk I/O.
