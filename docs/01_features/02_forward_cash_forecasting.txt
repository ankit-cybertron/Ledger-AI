===============================================================================
LEDGER AI - FORWARD CASH FORECASTER TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
===============================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Forward Cash Forecaster (`forecasting/engine.py`) predicts 30-to-90 day cash 
flow trajectories, estimates settlement dates for open payment gateway batches, 
overlays recurring cash flow patterns, and computes liquid cash availability 
starting from the Beginning Balance ($B_0$).

Core Philosophy:
  1. Observed Historical Settlement Lag: Near-term expected gateway settlements are 
     computed using empirical channel settlement cycles (e.g. Razorpay T+2 lag, 
     Stripe T+3 lag) derived from historical statement data.
  2. Deterministic Pattern Detection: Recurring cash debits/credits (payroll, 
     retainers, subscription batches) are identified using string similarity 
     and calendar interval matching.
  3. Seasonal Decomposition & WMA Projection: Baseline daily cash flows are 
     decomposed into trend and weekly seasonal components, projected using a 14-day 
     weighted moving average with confidence bands.
  4. Beginning Balance Integration: Cumulative cash balance series start from 
     the globally configured Beginning Balance ($B_0$).


2. MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATIONS & FORMULAS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Daily Net Cash Flow & Cumulative Balance:
   Transactions are aggregated by calendar day d:
     Net Cash Flow(d) = Sum(Deposits(d)) - Sum(|Payments(d)|)

   Cumulative liquid cash balance C(d) starts from Beginning Balance B_0:
     C(d) = B_0 + Sum_{k=d_0}^{d} Net Cash Flow(k)

b) Pending Gateway Settlement Estimation:
   For open gateway batches, expected settlement date d_expected is computed as:
     d_expected = d_batch + L_channel

   Default channel lags L_channel:
     - Razorpay: T + 2 business days
     - Stripe / Card Gateways: T + 3 business days
     - UPI Direct: T + 0 / T + 1 business days

c) Seasonal Decomposition & Moving Average Forecasting:
   Daily net inflows are decomposed into trend T(d) and weekly seasonal component 
   S(d) (7-day period):
     Trend(d) = RollingMean_7(NetInflow(d))

   Future projections for day t > today combine last trend level, seasonal component, 
   recurring pattern overlay, and expected pending settlements:
     Projected(t) = T_last + S(t mod 7) + PatternOverlay(t) + PendingSettlement(t)

   Confidence bands are calculated using standard deviation of residuals sigma:
     Upper Band(t) = Projected(t) + 1.5 * sigma * sqrt(t / 7)
     Lower Band(t) = Projected(t) - 1.5 * sigma * sqrt(t / 7)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) GET /api/forecast
   Query Parameters:
     - days (optional, default: 30, max: 90): Forecast horizon in days.

   Response Schema:

       "ok": true,
       "summary": {
